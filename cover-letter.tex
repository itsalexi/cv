Dear Recruiting Team,
\para

I am applying for the 2027 Asia Analyst Development Program Summer Analyst position in the Philippines. I am a Computer Science undergraduate at Ateneo de Manila University graduating in May 2028, and for the past year I have built financial products for Philippine businesses. I would like to spend my career on the systems and operations that sit behind financial services, and a rotational program is how I would rather learn them than by choosing a specialism now.
\para

At NextPay, a Y Combinator-backed payments company serving Philippine SMEs, I led the migration of the accounts receivable and invoicing platform across eight client-facing surfaces without interrupting live merchant workflows. I benchmarked the invoice and dashboard journeys our merchants repeat daily and cut median API calls by 15.6\% and workflow duration by 4.5\%. The result I am most pleased with, though, was eliminating a class of duplicate and failed requests that had been showing merchants inconsistent figures. In a payments product, a number that is occasionally wrong costs far more trust than one that loads slowly, and learning to tell those two problems apart has shaped how I prioritise since.
\para

My current role at Swarm is closer to controls work. I am building a compliance engine that maps 2,711 requirements drawn from seven privacy and security frameworks --- among them GDPR, the Philippine Data Privacy Act, and NIST CSF 2.0 --- into a single cross-framework graph, with 2,182 scored links between equivalent controls so that evidence gathered once can be reused rather than collected again. Most of the work is careful reading at volume and deciding what standard of proof is good enough before anything enters the corpus.
\para

Outside of work, I served as AVP for IT Skills and Development at the Ateneo Management Information Systems Association, where I ran a four-part development workshop series for over twenty students and replaced our manual event sign-in with a system that cut check-in time by 75\% across 525 members. I also build and maintain a suite of tools used by roughly 35,000 Ateneo students, which I support on my own through peak enlistment periods when any failure is visible to the entire university within minutes.
\para

My interest in J.P. Morgan is specific to this country. The firm has operated in the Philippines since 1961, and the Global Service Centers in Manila and Cebu mean that operations, technology, and controls for the wider firm are genuinely run from here rather than merely observed from here. I would like to learn those functions where they are actually performed, and I am glad the program rotates across revenue and non-revenue divisions --- I would be comfortable being placed anywhere from front to back office.
\para

I am a Filipino citizen and require no visa sponsorship, I speak Filipino and English, and I would be fully available for the full nine weeks. Thank you for your consideration.
\para\para
