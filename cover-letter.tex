Dear Recruiting Team,
\para

I am applying for the 2027 Asia Analyst Development Program Summer Analyst role in the Philippines. I study Computer Science at Ateneo de Manila University and graduate in May 2028. For the past year I have been building financial products for Philippine businesses, and the parts I care about most sit outside the code: how money moves, why a process breaks, and what a wrong number costs the person looking at it.
\para

At NextPay, a Y Combinator backed payments company serving Philippine SMEs, I led the migration of the accounts receivable and invoicing platform across eight client-facing surfaces without interrupting live merchant workflows. I benchmarked the journeys our merchants repeat every day, then cut median API calls by 15.6\%. The fix I am most pleased with was the least visible: duplicate and failed requests had been quietly showing merchants inconsistent figures, and I removed them. A number that is occasionally wrong costs far more trust than one that loads slowly.
\para

My current role at Swarm is closer to controls work. I am building a compliance engine that maps 2,711 requirements from seven privacy and security frameworks, among them GDPR, the Philippine Data Privacy Act, and NIST CSF 2.0, into one graph with 2,182 scored links between equivalent controls, so evidence gathered once can be reused. Most of the work is reading carefully at volume and deciding what standard of proof is good enough before anything is allowed in.
\para

Outside of work, I served as AVP for IT Skills and Development at the Ateneo Management Information Systems Association, where I ran a four-part workshop series for more than twenty students and replaced our manual event sign-in with a system that cut check-in time by 75\% across 525 members. I also build and maintain a set of student tools that have served roughly 35,000 users, which I run on my own through enlistment week, when anything that breaks is visible across campus in minutes.
\para

My interest in J.P. Morgan is specific to this country. The firm has been here since 1961, and the Global Service Centers in Manila and Cebu mean that operations, technology, and controls for the wider firm are genuinely run from the Philippines rather than only observed from it. I would rather learn those functions where the work actually happens, and I am glad the program rotates across divisions, since I would be happy to be placed anywhere from front to back office.
\para

I would be available for the full nine weeks. Thank you for your time, and I would welcome the chance to talk further.
\para
